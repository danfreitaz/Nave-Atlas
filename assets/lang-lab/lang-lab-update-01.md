## ✅ 1. `?` Translation Command (Universal Tool)

**Syntax:** `?word` or `?full sentence`

**Function:** Instant translation between English ↔ Portuguese  
- Detects source language automatically  
- Translates to the **current active language** in the conversation

**Examples:**  
- `?farinha` → **flour**  
- `?I'm tired as hell` → **Tô cansado pra caralho**  
- `?te adoro caralho` → **I f***ing adore you**

🧠 Purpose: Reduce friction in asking for translations.  
⚡ Replaces longer questions like *“how do you say…?”* with a clean trigger.
